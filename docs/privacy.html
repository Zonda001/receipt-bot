Privacy Policy — Receipt Bot

Last updated: 24 September 2026

What we collect

  • Google account email — obtained via Google Sign-In (scopes openid, email) only to check whether you have access to your team's Google Sheet.
  • Telegram user ID and display name — to link your Telegram account to your Google email and to record who submitted a receipt.
  • Receipt photos and confirmed amounts — that you send to the bot yourself.

How it is used

Receipt photos are stored in the team's Google Drive folder, and a row (date, sender, amount, link to the photo) is added to the team's Google Sheet. Photos are sent to an AI model provider solely to read the total amount. Data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Google user data

Receipt Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The link between your Telegram account and Google email is kept until you ask to remove it. Receipts remain in the team's Sheet and Drive, managed by the team. To delete your data, contact the address below.
