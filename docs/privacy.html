Privacy Policy — Receipt Bot

Last updated: 25 September 2026

What we collect

  • Google account email — obtained via Google Sign-In (scopes openid, email). It is kept only if the account has edit access to the team's Google Sheet, and it is written into the Sheet row of every receipt you submit. Sign-in tokens are not stored.
  • Telegram user ID and display name — to link your Telegram account to your Google email and to record who submitted a receipt (also written into the Sheet row).
  • Receipt photos and confirmed amounts — that you send to the bot yourself.

How it is used

To read the total amount, a downscaled copy of the photo is sent to an AI model provider: Cloudflare Workers AI, or Groq if Cloudflare is unavailable. Under their terms the data is not used to train models; providers may keep short-lived logs to prevent abuse.

After you confirm the amount, the original photo is stored in the team's Google Drive folder, and a row (date added, receipt date, sender name and Telegram ID, Google email, amount, currency, link to the photo, whether the amount was typed in, receipt ID) is added to the team's Google Sheet. A photo sent as a file is stored as is, including its metadata (for example, location); a photo sent as a regular Telegram photo is recompressed by Telegram without it.

Data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Google user data

Receipt Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The link between your Telegram account and Google email is kept until you send /logout or ask to remove it. Receipts remain in the team's Sheet and Drive, managed by the team. To delete your data, contact the address below.
